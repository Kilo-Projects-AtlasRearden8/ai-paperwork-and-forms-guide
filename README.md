# AI Paperwork and Forms Guide

A beginner-friendly manual for using AI to handle everyday paperwork: reading forms, filling out applications, benefits documents, leases, and insurance papers — plus what never to share and how to save copies.

Plain English. Privacy first. No medical advice, no legal advice, no secrets.

## Who this is for

This guide is written by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026). It's part of the Asdale Dynamics beginner AI education line — written "like a friend would explain it": step by step, never condescending, and free of jargon where possible.

If you've ever stared at a form and wondered where to start, this is for you.

## Privacy first

AI is helpful, but it is not a safe place for secrets. This guide includes a "what never to share" section and a privacy checklist. Read those before you upload anything.
