6. Insurance documents

Insurance paperwork — policies, claims, and explanation of benefits (EOB) statements — can be dense. AI can help you understand what's covered, what you owe, and how to file a claim. AI cannot give insurance advice or guarantee a claim will be paid.

Reading an insurance policy

Ask AI to help you find:

  • "What does this policy cover?"
  • "What is the deductible — how much do I pay before insurance pays?"
  • "What is the monthly premium?"
  • "What is not covered (exclusions)?"
  • "How do I file a claim?"
  • "What is the deadline to file a claim?"

Then check the policy yourself. Coverage limits and exclusions are usually in clearly labeled sections.

Reading a claim or explanation of benefits

An EOB shows what a provider charged, what insurance paid, and what you may owe. Ask AI:

  • "What amount am I responsible for?"
  • "Was this claim approved or denied?"
  • "Why was this denied, if it was?"
  • "What is the deadline to appeal?"
  • "What do I need to resubmit?"

Filing a claim

AI can help you draft:

  • A claim description: what happened, when, and where
  • A list of documents to attach (receipts, photos, police reports, medical records)
  • A follow-up letter if you haven't heard back

Keep your claim factual and include dates, policy numbers, and reference numbers.

Example: reviewing an EOB

You upload an explanation of benefits (with personal details removed) and ask:

"This EOB shows a charge I don't understand. Was it approved, and do I owe anything?"

AI might reply:

  • The claim was partially approved.
  • Insurance paid $120 of a $200 charge.
  • You may owe $80, depending on your deductible.

You check the original EOB for the exact patient responsibility amount and the appeal deadline.

Types of insurance documents this guide touches on

  • Health insurance policies and EOBs
  • Auto insurance policies and claims
  • Renters or homeowners insurance
  • Life insurance (general concepts only)

Safety reminders

  • Never paste your policy number, member ID, claim number, or full medical details into an AI chat.
  • Remove names, dates of birth, and addresses before sharing documents with AI.
  • Insurance decisions can affect your money and health coverage. Review important documents with your insurer, a benefits counselor, or a licensed agent.

Reminder. This guide is educational only. It is not insurance, medical, or legal advice. Coverage rules vary by state, insurer, and plan. Contact your insurance company or a licensed agent for help with your specific policy or claim.
