Privacy checklist

Use this checklist before you share any document or question with AI. If you answer "yes" to any of the "never share" items, remove that information first.

Before you upload

  • I removed or blacked out names, addresses, and phone numbers.
  • I removed or blacked out Social Security numbers.
  • I removed or blacked out driver's license, passport, or ID numbers.
  • I removed or blacked out bank account and credit card numbers.
  • I removed or blacked out passwords and PINs.
  • I removed or blacked out full medical records and diagnoses.
  • I removed or blacked out case numbers, claim numbers, and policy numbers.
  • I replaced real details with placeholders like [ADDRESS] or [DATE] where helpful.
  • I am only uploading pages I actually need help with.
  • I checked the AI tool's privacy settings (chat history, data training).
  • I know how to delete the chat when I'm done.

After you finish

  • I saved the original document and the AI's answer to my own files (see Chapter 8).
  • I reviewed the original document myself before signing or submitting anything.
  • I deleted the AI chat if the tool allows it.
  • If I shared anything sensitive by mistake, I took steps to protect myself (bank, credit monitoring, password change).

If something goes wrong

  • Financial info shared: contact your bank or card issuer immediately.
  • SSN or ID shared: consider credit monitoring and report at IdentityTheft.gov.
  • Password shared: change that password everywhere you use it.

Remember. This checklist is educational only. It is not legal or security advice. When in doubt, do not upload the document. Ask a qualified professional for help.
