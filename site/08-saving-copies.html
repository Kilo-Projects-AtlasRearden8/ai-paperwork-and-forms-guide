8. Saving and organizing your copies

AI chats are not a filing cabinet. Save your important paperwork yourself, in a place you control.

Where to save copies

Use a location you own and can back up:

  • A folder on your computer
  • A USB flash drive or external hard drive
  • A cloud storage account you control (Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud, OneDrive)

Do not rely on an AI chat history as your only copy. Chats can be deleted, accounts can be locked, and providers can change their terms.

What to save for each paperwork task

Save these five things:

  1. The original document — blank form, letter, lease, policy, or EOB
  2. Your notes — what you asked AI, the date, and which tool you used
  3. The AI's answer — copy-paste the response into a note or document
  4. Your final version — the filled form or letter you actually submitted
  5. Confirmation — submission receipt, email confirmation, or screenshot

Naming files

Use clear names with dates so you can find things later:

  • 2026-10-27_rental-application_blank.pdf
  • 2026-10-27_lease_ai-notes.txt
  • 2026-10-27_insurance-EOB_page1_redacted.pdf

A good pattern: YYYY-MM-DD_topic_description.ext

Organizing by topic

Create folders like:

paperwork/
  benefits/
  housing/
  insurance/
  jobs/
  taxes/

Backing up

  • Copy your paperwork folder to a second location (external drive or different cloud account) at least once a month.
  • If a document is important, keep both a digital copy and a printed copy.

Redacting before saving

If you save a document that contains secrets, create a redacted copy:

  • Black out SSNs, account numbers, and medical details
  • Save the redacted version as a new file: ..._redacted.pdf
  • Keep the unredacted original in a more secure location

Reminder. This guide is educational only. It is not legal or security advice. For important documents, keep copies in a place you control and review originals yourself.
