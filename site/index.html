Welcome

This guide walks you through using AI to handle everyday paperwork — reading forms, filling out applications, benefits documents, leases, and insurance papers — plus what never to share and how to save your copies.

Written in plain English by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana, formed October 2026). Privacy first. No medical advice, no legal advice.

How to use this guide

  • Read Chapter 1 first if you've never used AI before.
  • Chapters 2–6 cover specific document types.
  • Read Chapter 7 before you upload anything.
  • Read Chapter 8 for saving and organizing your copies.

A quick privacy rule

If you wouldn't show it to a stranger, don't paste it into an AI chat. Read Chapter 7 before you upload anything.

Educational only. This guide is not legal, medical, financial, or insurance advice. When paperwork affects your rights, money, or health, review it with a qualified professional.
