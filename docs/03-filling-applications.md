# 3. Filling out applications

AI can help you draft answers for job, benefit, housing, and other applications. You still review and submit everything yourself.

## How to use AI to fill out an application

### Step 1: Gather what you need

Before you start, collect:

- The blank application (or a link to it)
- Your résumé or work history
- Any reference names and contact information
- The job or program description, if applying for something specific

### Step 2: Ask AI to draft answers

Share the application questions (without secrets — see Chapter 7) and ask:

- "Here is a job application. It asks 'Why do you want this job?' The job is a receptionist role at a clinic. I have two years of nursing experience. Can you draft a short answer?"
- "This application asks for my availability. I can work mornings and weekends. Can you write a one-sentence answer?"
- "I need to list three professional references. What should I include for each one?"

### Step 3: Personalize the draft

AI drafts can sound generic. Rewrite in your own voice so the answer sounds like you:

- Add specific details only you know
- Remove anything that isn't true
- Keep answers concise — most applications prefer short responses

### Step 4: Copy into the application

Copy your final answer into the application form. Do not let AI submit the application for you. Review each field before you click submit.

## Example: drafting an application answer

Application question: "Describe a time you handled a difficult situation at work."

AI draft:

> "In my previous role as a nurse, a patient became upset about a wait time. I listened calmly, explained the delay, and offered to check on them sooner. They calmed down, and we finished the visit without further issues."

Your personalized version:

> "While working as a registered nurse, a patient was frustrated about a long wait. I listened without interrupting, explained the reason for the delay, and offered to have a team member check on them within fifteen minutes. They calmed down, and we completed the appointment smoothly."

## Common application questions AI can help with

- "Why do you want this position?"
- "Tell us about yourself."
- "What are your strengths and weaknesses?"
- "Describe a challenge and how you handled it."
- "Why are you leaving your current role?"
- "What salary are you looking for?" (research local pay ranges first — AI doesn't know your local market)

## Safety reminders

- Do not paste your Social Security number, driver's license number, or bank account details into an AI chat.
- Do not paste your current employer's confidential information.
- Review Chapter 7 before sharing anything personal.

## Reminder

This guide is educational only. It is not career, legal, or financial advice. Review your completed application yourself before submitting.
