# 1. Getting started with AI for paperwork

You do not need to be technical to use AI for paperwork. If you can type a question into a search box, you can use this guide.

## What AI can do with paperwork

AI can help you:

- Read a long form and explain it in plain language
- Point out important dates, fees, and signatures needed
- Draft answers for an application you need to fill out
- Compare two documents side by side
- Make a checklist of what you still need to do

## What AI cannot do

AI cannot:

- Sign legal documents for you
- Give legal, medical, or financial advice
- Guarantee a form is correct or complete
- Keep your data private by default — you must protect it yourself

## Picking an AI tool

You have a few options. Choose what feels safest and easiest for you.

| Option | Good for | Privacy note |
|--------|----------|--------------|
| Web chat (e.g., a free AI assistant website) | Quick one-off questions | Your chats may be stored by the provider |
| Desktop app with local AI | Sensitive paperwork | Runs on your computer; nothing leaves your device |
| Paid AI assistant | Regular use, better features | Check the provider's privacy policy |

If you are unsure, start with a web chat for non-sensitive practice forms (see Chapter 2 for practice ideas). Move to local AI before you upload anything with personal details.

## Starting a chat

1. Open the AI tool.
2. Paste or type your question. Example: "I have a rental application. Can you explain what this section means in simple words?"
3. If the tool lets you upload files, you can upload a PDF or image of the form.
4. Read the AI's answer carefully. AI can make mistakes. Always check the original document yourself.

## A safe way to practice

Before you use AI on real paperwork, try it on something harmless:

- Take a blank sample form (many government websites offer blank templates).
- Upload it and ask AI to walk you through each field.
- Do not fill in real names, addresses, or numbers yet.

This builds your confidence without risking your privacy.

## Keep a paper trail

Whenever AI helps you with paperwork, save:

- The original document (blank or filled)
- A note of what you asked the AI
- The AI's answer
- The date you worked on it

See Chapter 8 for how to save and organize copies.

## Reminder

This guide is educational only. It is not legal advice. If a document affects your rights, money, or housing, have a qualified professional review it before you sign.
