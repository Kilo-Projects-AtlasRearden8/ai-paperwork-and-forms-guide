# AI Paperwork and Forms Guide

Welcome. This manual walks you through using AI to handle everyday paperwork — reading forms, filling out applications, benefits documents, leases, and insurance papers — plus what never to share and how to save your copies.

Written in plain English by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**. Privacy first. No medical advice, no legal advice.

## How to use this manual

Each chapter covers one kind of paperwork. Read Chapter 1 first if you've never used AI before. Chapters 2–6 cover specific document types. Chapters 7 and 8 cover privacy and saving your work — read those before you upload anything.

You can read this manual here (`docs/`) or as a static website in `site/`. Just open any `.html` file in your browser; nothing needs to be installed.

## Chapters

| Chapter | What it covers |
|---------|----------------|
| 1. Getting started | Picking an AI tool, starting a chat, basic safety |
| 2. Reading forms | Uploading a form, asking AI to explain it in plain language |
| 3. Filling out applications | Drafting answers, reviewing before you submit |
| 4. Benefits documents | Understanding benefit letters, appeals, and applications |
| 5. Leases and rental agreements | Spotting key clauses, questions to ask your landlord |
| 6. Insurance documents | Reading policies, claims, and explanation of benefits |
| 7. What never to share | Privacy rules — SSN, passwords, medical details, and more |
| 8. Saving and organizing copies | Where to keep files, naming, backups |

## A quick privacy rule to remember

**If you wouldn't show it to a stranger, don't paste it into an AI chat.** Read Chapter 7 before you upload anything.
