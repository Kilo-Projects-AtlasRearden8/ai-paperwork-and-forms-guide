# 7. What never to share

AI is a helpful tool, but it is not a safe place for secrets. Anything you paste into an AI chat might be stored, reviewed by people, or used to train future models — depending on the provider's rules. Treat every AI chat like a public conversation.

## Never share these with AI

| Never share | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| Social Security number | Identity theft risk |
| Full date of birth | Identity theft risk |
| Driver's license, passport, or ID numbers | Identity theft risk |
| Bank account or credit card numbers | Financial fraud risk |
| Passwords or PINs | Account takeover risk |
| Full medical records or diagnoses | Privacy and discrimination risk |
| Your home address | Safety and privacy risk |
| Phone number and email | Spam and phishing risk |
| Employer confidential information | Job and legal risk |
| Case numbers, claim numbers, or policy numbers | Fraud and privacy risk |

## How to share safely

Before you upload a document or paste text into AI:

1. **Remove or black out** names, addresses, phone numbers, SSNs, account numbers, and medical details.
2. **Use fake details** if you need AI to understand the structure: "My address is [ADDRESS]" instead of your real address.
3. **Upload only what you need.** If you only need help with one page, share only that page.
4. **Check the AI tool's settings.** Some tools let you disable chat history or data training. Turn those on if available.
5. **Delete chats** when you're done, if the tool allows it.

## What IS okay to share

- Blank forms with no personal information filled in
- General questions: "What does a security deposit usually cover?"
- Fake example details you made up
- Public information already available online

## If you accidentally shared something sensitive

1. Delete the chat from the AI tool, if possible.
2. If it was a financial number, contact your bank or card issuer.
3. If it was an SSN or ID, consider monitoring your credit and reporting to the FTC at IdentityTheft.gov.
4. Change any passwords you shared.

## A simple rule to remember

If you wouldn't show it to a stranger sitting next to you in a waiting room, don't paste it into an AI chat.

## Reminder

This guide is educational only. It is not legal or security advice. For serious privacy concerns, speak with a qualified professional.
